Who we are

Built on expertise, driven by precision.

E-HUB Engineering Consultancy, short for Engineering Hub, is a Pokhara-based firm founded by licensed surveyors and engineers committed to accurate, government-standard land and engineering survey services across Nepal.

We combine modern equipment with deep local knowledge of Gandaki Province's terrain, land records, and government processes to serve individuals, developers, and institutions.

Our goal is simple: give every client clear, honest, and precise results on time and within budget.

What we offer

Core survey and engineering services.

Professional services delivered to government and international standards.

TS

Topographic survey

Accurate mapping of natural and man-made terrain features, elevation data, and land profiles for planning and design.

CM

Contour mapping

Detailed contour line production to represent slope, drainage patterns, and elevation changes across any site.

LS

Land & cadastral survey

Boundary surveys, land measurements, plot verification, subdivision planning, and cadastral mapping services.

AV

Avilekhikaran

Official land record documentation, lalpurja mapping, plot verification, and government-standard avilekhikaran services.

CAD

Engineering design & planning

Road, site, and infrastructure planning with modern engineering software including Civil 3D and AutoCAD.

DPR

DPR & feasibility study

Preparation of Detailed Project Reports, feasibility studies, technical reports, and project documentation.

2D

2D drafting & design

Professional CAD drawings, site layouts, floor plans, and engineering drawings for various projects.

3D

3D modeling & visualization

3D terrain models, corridor modeling, surface analysis, and realistic project visualizations.

RD

Road design & Civil 3D services

Road alignment design, profile design, cross-sections, corridor modeling, quantity estimation, and roadway engineering using Civil 3D.

BOQ

Quantity estimation & BOQ preparation

Preparation of quantity estimates, BOQs, cost estimates, rate analysis, and tender documentation.

PM

Construction & construction supervision

Site execution support, technical supervision, quality control, progress monitoring, contractor coordination, and project management.

CS

Construction survey & setting out

Building layout, road centerline setting out, leveling, and construction staking services.

RE

Real estate & land services

Complete real estate survey and legal services including land measurement, plot development, plot sketch preparation, map verification, and documentation assistance.

VAL
